# Product

## Register

product

## Users
PMs on an internal team. They use this tool during their workday — at a desk,
in a browser tab alongside other tools — to draft, review, and publish release
announcements. No external users; no auth. They are in execution mode when they
open it: something needs to go out.

## Product Purpose
Release Hub lets PMs create and publish release announcements without depending
on the frontend team. PMs author content, route it through an approval workflow
(draft → pending → approved → published → archived), and the renderer delivers
the announcement as an embedded widget in other products. Success is a published
release that reached its audience without a ticket to engineering.

## Brand Personality
Focused, direct, unobtrusive. The tool does not call attention to itself. It
presents information clearly and moves the user toward the next action without
friction or flourish.

## Anti-references
- Typical AI product aesthetics: purple gradients, floating orbs, glassmorphism,
  hero-metric dashboards, SaaS-cream with soft shadows everywhere.
- Maximalism: visual noise, decorative elements, animation-heavy interfaces,
  multiple competing focal points, color used for atmosphere rather than meaning.

## Design Principles
1. Infrastructure over identity. The tool disappears; the released content is
   the product. No surface should draw attention to itself.
2. Signal over noise. Every visual element earns its presence. Decoration is a
   cost, not a feature.
3. One voice at a time. No competing focal points. Clarity through constraint.
4. Color means something. Violet marks a decision point. Status colors mark a
   lifecycle state. Nothing else gets saturated color.
5. Legibility at speed. PMs scan; they do not read. The interface serves
   the scan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA. No known specific user needs. Reduced-motion preference respected for
any transitions.
